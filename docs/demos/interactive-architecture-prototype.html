1. Keep broadcast and inference separate

fal produces or transforms media; a dedicated origin and CDN deliver one continuous program to the audience.

2. Commerce remains authoritative

Prices, inventory, promotions and orders come from deterministic services, never from generated model output.

3. Hide generation latency with a buffer

Prepare two or more approved segments ahead of air, reject stale results, and always carry a safe fallback.

4. Design for graceful degradation

An AI outage must not interrupt playback, chat, cart, checkout or order creation.
